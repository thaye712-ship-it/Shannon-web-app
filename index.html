About Shannon

Shannon is an internal training tool built for Design Within Reach retail teams. It exists to close one specific gap: associates are expected to speak knowledgeably about the designers, history, materials and construction behind DWR's assortment — knowledge that normally builds slowly, on the floor, over months. Shannon compresses it into short, repeatable sessions instead.

What it teaches

For each piece: who designed it, what manufacturer makes it, what year it was introduced, the design movement it belongs to, what it is made of, and a short piece of history worth repeating to a customer. A separate materials and construction glossary covers vocabulary that spans the assortment — cantilever frames, molded plywood, cane webbing, tubular steel, powder coating, outdoor teak — so an associate can explain how a piece is made, not only who made it.

Who it's for

Sales associates

A five-minute daily habit or a longer onboarding session that builds the product knowledge a floor conversation depends on — without sitting through a manual or a slideshow.

Store & regional management

A consistent, repeatable way to raise product knowledge across a team, especially for new hires, without building or running a training program from scratch.

Current status

First batch: 30 products, 14 construction & materials terms Roughly a quarter of the eventual catalog, covering the design classics associates are asked about most. Content is researched from published, verifiable design history — not pulled or scraped from dwr.com — and more is being added the same way on a rolling basis.

Data & access

  • No login or account — anyone with the link can train.
  • Progress (XP, level, streak, badges) is stored on-device only, in the browser. It does not sync across devices.
  • There is no manager dashboard yet, so completion cannot currently be tracked across a team.
  • Not connected to dwr.com — no live pricing, inventory, or content is pulled from the production site.
