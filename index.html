About Provenance

Provenance is an internal training tool built for Design Within Reach retail teams. It exists to close one specific gap: associates are expected to speak knowledgeably about the designers, history, materials and construction behind DWR's assortment — knowledge that normally builds slowly, on the floor, over months. Provenance compresses it into short, repeatable sessions instead.

The name is the point. A piece's provenance is where it came from — who drew it, who builds it, what year, what it's made of. That's exactly what an associate needs on hand when a customer asks why it costs what it costs.

What it teaches

For each piece: who designed it, what manufacturer makes it, what year it was introduced, the design movement it belongs to, what it is made of, and a short piece of history worth repeating to a customer — plus recognizing it on sight, since a Photo ID question and the flashcard deck both use the real product photo. A separate materials and construction glossary covers vocabulary that spans the assortment — cantilever frames, molded plywood, cane webbing, tubular steel, powder coating, outdoor teak — so an associate can explain how a piece is made, not only who made it.

Who it's for

Sales associates

A five-minute daily habit or a longer onboarding session that builds the product knowledge a floor conversation depends on — without sitting through a manual or a slideshow.

Store & regional management

A consistent, repeatable way to raise product knowledge across a team, especially for new hires, without building or running a training program from scratch.

Current status

121 products, 20 construction & materials terms Spanning seating, sofas, tables, desks, lighting, storage, bedroom and outdoor. Every product is sourced from DWR's own listings, so the training set matches what DWR actually sells. Variants of one design are deliberately collapsed into a single entry.

Data & access

  • Profiles need only a name — no password, no email. Anyone can make one, and each person keeps their own progress, so a shared showroom device works for a whole team.
  • Progress (XP, level, streak, badges) is stored on that device only. It does not follow someone to their phone.
  • There is no manager dashboard yet, so completion cannot currently be tracked across a team.
  • Product photos are hotlinked directly from DWR's own image hosting so the Photo ID quiz and flashcards show the real piece, but no pricing, inventory, or other content is pulled from the production site.
